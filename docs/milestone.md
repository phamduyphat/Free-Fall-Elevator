# SREMS Mode 2 (ACS) & Mode 5 (Analytics) Milestone Brief

## 1. Mode 2: Access Control & Security (ACS) — Completed

### 1.1 Domain & Database Implementation (`acs/`, `db.py`)
- `SEC-2.1` ("Simulated card identity. The system shall represent an access card with a unique credential identifier linked to a user identity and permission set."): Implemented PostgreSQL `users` and `credentials` persistence with unique `credential_id` records in `acs/credential_accesscontrol.py` and `db.py`.
- `SEC-2.2` ("Role-based authorization. The system shall enforce separate permissions for passengers, technicians, building-service staff, and Admins (Security manager and Building owner)."): Implemented `UserRole` and `DASHBOARD_PERMISSIONS` RBAC policy matrices across `acs/enums.py`, `acs/cab_accesscontrol.py`, `acs/dashboard_accesscontrol.py`, and `acs/fleet_accesscontrol.py`.
- `SEC-2.3` ("Resident floor policy. By default, a resident may select the apartment floor assigned to the resident and configured common floors."): Enforced default Floor 1, B1, and assigned floor resolution in `parse_allowed_floors` in `acs/helpers.py`.
- `SEC-2.4` ("Hall-call exception. Hall-call submission shall remain available without passenger authentication.") & `SEC-2.5` ("B1 exception. A request for B1 shall be accepted without a card, subject to residential elevator availability and defined emergency/maintenance restrictions."): Implemented cardless B1 destination acceptance on residential cars `E1`–`E4` in `CabAccessControl.validate_car_destination`.
- `FR-1.13` ("Freight/service elevator. E5 shall not be assigned to B1. Residents and staff may request E5..."): Blocked `E5` destination and service-run requests targeting B1 (`E5_B1_FORBIDDEN`) while gating `E5` service runs in `CabAccessControl.validate_e5_service_run`.
- `SEC-2.6` ("Authorization denial. An unauthorized destination command shall be rejected before it reaches the elevator interface."): Rejected unauthorized floor selections with `AuthorizationResult.deny` before dispatch and logged denials in `access_authorization_events` via `acs/logs.py`.
- `SEC-2.7` ("Repeated failure detection. ... The system shall generate an alert when the number of failed attempts reaches a configurable threshold."): Implemented a 3-failures-in-60-seconds sliding counter that emits `REPEATED_FAILURES` security alerts in `record_failed_attempt` (`acs/helpers.py`).
- `SEC-2.8` ("Security dashboard alerts. Security alerts shall include severity, timestamp, relevant user/card identifier where available, elevator, attempted action, result, and reason."): Implemented structured alert creation, filtering, and status updates (`OPEN`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `RESOLVED`) in `Logging` (`acs/logs.py`).
- `SEC-2.9` ("Control-command permissions. Commands that change elevator availability, maintenance state, emergency state, or access policy shall require an authorized role and shall be audited."): Validated and audited maintenance, return-to-service, emergency override, and rescue dispatch commands in `FleetAccessControl`.
- `SEC-2.10` ("External input validation. All incoming elevator messages shall be validated for recognized message type, known elevator, valid floor range (B1-30), legal state value, and permissible state transition before acceptance."): Rejected unknown elevator IDs, out-of-range floors, and invalid operation types as `MALFORMED_MSG_REJECTED` via `reject_malformed_msg`.
- `SEC-2.11` ("Security audit events. The system shall record security-sensitive actions including successful/failed authentication, destination denial, permission changes, maintenance isolation, emergency commands, manual overrides, and rejected external messages."): Centralized immutable bulk-capable audit persistence (`AuditEntry` in `acs/payloads.py`) across all `AuditActionType` values in `Logging.write_audit`.
- `SEC-2.12` ("Audit log protection. Audit records shall be protected against unauthorized modification or deletion after creation. Any unauthorized modification or removal shall be detectable through an integrity-verification mechanism.") & `OR-11` ("Tamper-evident implementation"): Implemented SHA-256 hash chaining (`audit_log_chain`) and first-mismatch detection in `Logging` and `DashboardAccessControl.run_audit_integrity_check`.
- `SEC-2.13` ("Least privilege. Users shall not invoke operations outside their assigned role and explicit permissions, regardless of which interface path is used."): Enforced per-route `authorize_dashboard_op` checks in `api/helpers.py` backed by `DashboardAccessControl.validate_dashboard_operation`.
- `SEC-2.14` ("Security investigation. Admins shall be able to search and review access denials, repeated failures, emergency events, and relevant audit records by time range, user/card, elevator, event type, and status where data is available."): Implemented parameterized PostgreSQL search methods in `Logging` for authorization events, audit logs, incidents, and security alerts.
- `SEC-2.15` ("Authentication method. Authentication shall use a credential (card identity) only... A dashboard session shall end after 15 minutes without activity (configurable)."): Implemented card-only authentication with opaque Bearer tokens and 900-second sliding TTL expiry in `DashboardAccessControl`.
- `SEC-2.16` ("Credential lifecycle. A credential shall be in one of the states ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, REVOKED, or EXPIRED."): Implemented `ISSUE`, `ISSUE_VISITOR_PASS`, `SUSPEND`, `REINSTATE`, and `REVOKE` state machine transitions in `CredentialAccessControl.manage_credential`.
- `SEC-2.17` ("Lost card. When a card holder reports a card lost, an Admin shall first suspend it (SUSPENDED, reason LOST) for a review window of 72 hours by default... For a lost Admin card, another Admin shall do so."): Enforced peer-admin checks, seeded two initial Admin cards (`ADMIN-CARD-0001` and `ADMIN-CARD-0002`) in `seed.sql` and `db.py`, implemented atomic `replace_lost_card`, raised first-attempt lost/revoked alerts, and added 72-hour bulk auto-revocation on clock ticks (`apply_bulk_credential_transitions`).
- `SEC-2.18` ("Duplicated or cloned card. If one credential identifier is presented in two different cars within 10 seconds (default, configurable), SREMS shall deny the later request, raise a security alert that names both cars, and set the credential to SUSPENDED until an Admin reviews it."): Implemented 10-second cross-car swipe tracking, automatic suspension (`CLONE_SUSPECTED`), and `CLONED_CREDENTIAL` critical alerts in `CabAccessControl._handle_if_cloned_swipe`.
- `SEC-2.19` ("Unregistered credential. A credential identifier that is not registered shall be denied for restricted destinations and counted as a failed attempt under SEC-2.7... If more than 10 distinct unregistered identifiers are presented within 5 minutes (default, configurable), SREMS shall also raise one security alert for suspected card scanning."): Tracked unregistered cards under `failed_attempts_counter` and emitted `CARD_SCANNING` alerts when >10 distinct unknown IDs occur within 300 seconds in `acs/helpers.py`.
- `SEC-2.20` ("Command ordering. Control commands that act on the same car shall be applied in the order SREMS receives them. A command that conflicts with the state left by an earlier command, for example a rescue dispatch for a car that is in maintenance, shall be rejected with a reason and audited (SEC-2.9)."): Validated non-E5 rescue car constraints (`E5_INELIGIBLE_AS_RESCUE_CAR`) and dwell wait bounds in `FleetAccessControl.validate_rescue_car_dispatch`.
- `SEC-2.21` ("One presentation, one destination. A credential presentation shall authorize exactly one restricted destination selection."): Enforced single-use per-car tokens (`ONE_TAP_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`) with 5-second (`RESIDENT`/`VISITOR`) and 30-second (`BUILDING_SERVICE`/`TECHNICIAN`) windows in `CabAccessControl`.
- `SEC-2.22` ("Visitor pass. At a resident's request, an Admin shall be able to issue a visitor pass: a credential limited to named floors that expires after a set time (default 4 hours)."): Implemented visitor pass issuance with 4-hour default TTL and audited `VISITOR_PASS_EXPIRED` transitions both on lookup (`acs/helpers.py`) and in bulk on every `SimulationClock` tick (`apply_bulk_credential_transitions`).
- `MNT-4.11` ("SREMS Failure.") & Fail-Closed Security (`VT-26`): Wrapped database-backed ACS transactions in `fail_closed` (`DB_FAIL_CLOSED`) handlers so uninitialized or failed PostgreSQL access denies restricted operations.

## 2. Mode 5: Analytics & Audit Management — Completed

### 2.1 Domain & KPI Engine (`analytics/`)
- `ANA-5.1` ("Historical event persistence. The system shall persist hall calls, car calls, assignments, trips, fault transitions, emergencies, communication-loss events, maintenance operations, authorization decisions, and security alerts."): Read-only KPI queries consume persisted PostgreSQL tables defined in `db.py` and `seed.sql`.
- `ANA-5.2` ("Wait-time metrics. The system shall report average, median, and percentile wait time by selected period and traffic profile when the underlying timestamp data is available.") & `PERF-9`/`PERF-10`: Implemented mean, median ($P_{50}$), $P_{90}$, $P_{95}$, drop rate, reassignment count, no-boarding count, and peak/off-peak target checks in `TrafficMetrics.get_wait_time_kpis`.
- `ANA-5.3` ("Travel-time metrics. The system shall report elevator travel time between recorded departures and arrivals when available."): Implemented physical car leg travel-time aggregation with `is_estimated` flag preservation in `TrafficMetrics.get_travel_time_kpis` and directional/B1/E5 trip shares in `TrafficMetrics.get_trip_distribution`.
- `ANA-5.4` ("Utilization metrics. The system shall report per-car trip count, starts, travelled distance or a supported equivalent, and time in operational/fault/maintenance states when those data can be obtained or derived consistently."): Implemented per-car trips, motor starts, traversed distance, and derived operational state durations (`IN_SERVICE`, `FAULT`, `MAINTENANCE`, `EMERGENCY`, `OCCUPIED`, `STALE`) in `FleetMetrics.get_utilization_kpis`.
- `ANA-5.5` ("Downtime. The system shall calculate each car’s recorded downtime from fault/maintenance start and end events. Intervals still open at report time shall be identified as ongoing.") & `AVL-1`: Implemented car downtime aggregation, ongoing interval tracking, and `system_heartbeats`-based SREMS availability calculation in `FleetMetrics.get_downtime_summary`.
- `ANA-5.6` ("Security metrics. The system shall summarize authorization denials, failed authentication attempts, repeated-failure alerts, external validation failures, and emergency incidents."): Implemented security KPI summaries across `access_authorization_events`, `security_alerts`, `audit_log_chain`, and `incidents` in `SecurityMetrics.get_security_kpis`.
- `ANA-5.7` ("Incident search. Authorized staff shall search incident and audit records by elevator, user/card, event type, status, and time range where those fields exist.") & `ANA-5.8` ("Audit integrity verification. An Admin shall be able to run audit-integrity verification and view whether the recorded chain is intact, including the first detected invalid record when one is found."): Delegated incident/audit filtering and SHA-256 integrity verification to `ACS.Logging` and `DashboardAccessControl`.
- `ANA-5.9` ("Report export. The system shall export per-request timestamps/outcomes and aggregate performance/reliability/security measures for a recorded run, including optional benchmark runs (Section 3.3.4)."): Implemented JSON and CSV report export with optional per-request call/trip rows and `REPORT_EXPORTED` audit logging in `ExportEngine.export_run_report`.
